What launch includes—and what happens next.

The pilot is one property and 30 days of automated guest text support, followed by a review—not an open-ended concierge commitment.

1

Before launch: set up and test

We configure property answers and an escalation contact with the host. The host reviews and approves them; together we test common questions, escalations, and SMS opt-out/help before guests receive the number.

2

During the pilot: support and learn

The host shares the guest-initiated text line. Automated replies use approved property information; uncertain questions are sent to the host, who remains responsible for follow-up. We review activity and feedback weekly to spot recurring needs.

3

At day 30: review and decide

We review usage, answer quality, escalations, and repeated guest needs. The host can stop or discuss a separately priced continuation. Future add-ons will only be offered after they are scoped, built, tested, and priced.

Ask about the pilot

A few practical questions

What the current pilot does—and what it does not promise.

What happens after the 30-day pilot?

We review usage and host feedback together. You can stop, or separately agree to continue at a price discussed before continuing. There is no automatic renewal and no long-term price set yet.

Does StayXI handle emergencies or provide 24/7 human coverage?

No. The system is designed to provide immediate safety guidance and notify the configured host for recognized emergencies, but it is not emergency dispatch or guaranteed human coverage. Call 911 for immediate danger.

How are answers and access details controlled?

Answers are based on information the host supplies and approves. Uncertain or higher-risk questions are routed away from AI. Access credentials are not sent to AI; critical changes are staged for an explicit review before publication.

Are future add-ons available now?

No. Guest-question insights, answer-library tune-ups, and portfolio trends are candidates to evaluate from pilot feedback—not current services or commitments. Any future add-on must be built, tested, and separately priced first.
