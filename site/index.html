How it works

About ten minutes of your time, once

1

Send the welcome book you already have

A PDF, a notes app, a listing link — whatever you hand guests today. The guide is drafted from it automatically.

2

Approve the facts, once

You correct anything wrong and sign off. From then on the line answers only from that approved guide, never from guesses.

3

Put the number in your confirmation

Guests text it and get an answer in seconds. Anything that needs a person arrives with the whole thread attached.

Questions, answered

  • It answers only from the guide you approved. If a question falls outside it — or touches money, damage, maintenance or a complaint — it stops and hands the guest to you rather than improvising. Every reply is logged against the guide section it came from, so you can always see how an answer was reached.

  • A short, honest message saying the question has been passed to the host, who will reply directly. The guest is never left in silence and never given a made-up answer to a real problem.

  • Yes. It introduces itself as the property assistant on the first message. Pretending otherwise is how a host ends up with the review nobody wants.

  • No. Guests text a separate number that StayXI provides, and you put it in the confirmation message. Your personal number stays private, and an existing business number can be ported later if you prefer.

  • No. This is an SMS line. Platform threads stay exactly where they are, for the questions that arrive inside the app. The text line covers the guest who has already left the platform, is driving, or will never open another app.

  • It is not an emergency service. Urgent wording — lockout, leak, smoke, no heat — is routed to you immediately. Guests should be told to call you for anything dangerous and to call 911 for anything life-threatening.

  • About ten minutes once we have the book: send the welcome guide you already give guests, correct anything the draft got wrong and approve it. We set up and test the line with you before any guest uses it. There is no self-serve signup yet.
